# Expand write-relay discovery logging

## Goal

Emit a structured operational diagnostic when the effective relay set used for
writable-cache publication is first discovered and whenever it changes as the
signer's NIP-65 outboxes are combined with configured extra relays.

## Tasks

1. Add a safe, formatted write-relay-list operational diagnostic that reports
   the effective endpoints and their configured/outbox source counts.
2. During write activation, follow the signer's relay metadata and subscribe to
   its distinct effective relay set, emitting `found` once and `changed` later.
3. Add integration coverage for relay discovery/change behavior and diagnostic
   formatting, then run the relevant tests and full verification.
