<decisions_made>

- One persistent hash-addressed store with a 16 GiB default hard ceiling counts physical unique bytes plus active reservations.
- Unowned cached remote blobs are LRU-evictable; durably owned or actively leased blobs are not.
- Write-origin blobs are deleted after their final durable owner and lease disappear rather than retained as cache entries.
- The decoded manifest LRU remains separate from the raw-byte store.
- NAR PUTs are chunked using the existing Hashtree chunk size and become visible only after complete transactional admission.
- `localBlossomUrl` is removed; preferred and publisher Blossom endpoints remain upstream sources.
- Legacy migration must be restart-safe and idempotent, adopting candidate ownership before staged routes.
- Existing commit `de3d233` is accepted intact; do not amend, split, or rewrite it.

</decisions_made>
